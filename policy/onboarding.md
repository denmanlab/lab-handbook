# Onboarding

## 1. Technology / Accounts
a. Choose what account you want to use for a Slack invite to join the Denman Lab Slack group

b. Make a GitHub account if you don't have one. send Dan your username and you can join the denmanlab organization. DO this even your work isn't obviously going to use any code to start with!

c. Log in to [elab][6] (only accessible on campus). You can create an account, choose "Denman Lab" for your team. 

it is recommended, but not at all required, that you use your university email (you can make a google account for your CU email) for 1a-c. 

d. [optional] Also ask Dan for a username and password for the NAS. This is used for accessing the data lab's shared data backup.

e. make sure you have the Denman Lab Outlook calendar through your CU email.


## 2. Read the Wiki pages!
Especially: 
- [Code of Conduct][7]
- [Expectations][8]
- [Code Management Practices][3]
- [Data Management][4]`

## 3. Complete animal qualifications
-- if you are to perform any animal work, review the protocol and provide Dan the information for you to be added to the protocol. [Personnel Qualification Form][1]. [Occupational Health Annual Update][2].

-- protocol is available through the lab Slack. [pinned in the Regulatory channel]

## 4. Complete University of Colorado required onboarding:
- sexual harassment training
- lab safety (link)
- animal training
- occupational health (medical surveillance)
- badge, if you don't have one
- others...


## 5. Personnel
--  [for staff positions] HR meeting, usually scheduled by HR for the day you start or day after

-- establish the schedule for 1-1 meetings with Dan, or project meetings

-- When joining the lab in full, you should complete and IDP and meet with Dan to develop it within the first ~6 months. [The IDP template is here][5].

# Code of Conduct

All members and visitors to the lab are expected to conduct themselves according to the following code of conduct:

**Safety**
<br>
All members are expected to ensure a safe working environment. This includes laboratory and personal safety. 
<br><br>
**Respect**
<br>
Respect must be shown to all others at all times. The lab will not tolerate hurtful or disrespectful speech or behavior of any kind. No member shall feel harassed because of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion (or lack thereof).

Research is an inherently changing process - we are *all* learning at all times. It is the duty of members of the lab of every level to create and maintain a supportive lab and learning culture. All members are expected encourage and entertain questions from anyone, no matter what prior you bring to the question or the person.
<br><br>
**Communication**
<br>
All members must make an effort to communicate any and all concerns to someone. This includes not only personal experiences, but *any* observations of *any* violations of this code of conduct.
<br><br>
**Waste**
<br>
Don't be wasteful! Waste generated by research is a serious problem that directly harms environmental and fiscal sustainability. All members are expected to consider how to reduce waste in materials, animals, and time without sacrificing the quality of their research. 
<br><br>
***Reporting Concerns:***
<br>
If you have any concerns about conduct in the lab, please report them to Dan. You can do this in any way that you feel comfortable - in person or in writing; identified or anonymous. 

Any concerns can also be reported directly to the [CU Anschutz Ombuds Office][1]. This office is an excellent resource, especially if you feel you cannot bring an issue to Dan. 


  [1]: https://www1.ucdenver.edu/offices/ombudsoffice

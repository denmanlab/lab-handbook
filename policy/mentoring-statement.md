# Mentoring Statement

## Dan's Mentorship Philosophy
My mentorship philosophy is centered on development of **independent** scientists. 

I view my role with all trainees as providing them the set of tools needed to grow - rarely if ever punitive, hopefully always responsive. 

    This typically means different things for each mentee, whether at the same or different career stages. It is an individualized approach, which in turn enforces a relatively small group of mentees at any given time. I believe this growth-minded, small-group approach is best-suited for all trainees. I strive give independence to a well-prepared student who needs to be challenged, while simultaneously working more closely with those who may not have yet learned a skill or been exposed to a part of science. 

Development towards independence requires multiple phases (not always linear, not always in a set order, and sometimes repeated) including exploration, execution, and critical evaluation. To complete these I strive creating clear expectations and plans in collaboration with the mentee. 

     Mentor and mentee are to write down timelines for expected development together and trade versions. Progress (scientific and professional) is evaluated on fixed schedules, as written by the mentee; both provide constructive feedback in writing so it can be referenced. We have regular meetings, with formal meeting agendas, and shared documentation of meeting notes.
     
     Bi-weekly Meeting agendas have the following four sections:
     1. Overall goal: this is a goal that is unlikely to change on a week to week basis, but seeing it is a reminder of what the mid-range goal is
     2. Weekly Goal Status: what your weekly goals were from last time, and whether they happened
     3. Weekly Goals: Goals for the upcoming week
     4. How Dan can help: What i can do to make weekly goals that were missed, or upcoming ones, happen.

### What trainees can expect from Dan

- I view my role as a mentor as providing the means and the support to meet those expectations.
- I strive to bring creativity and joy to the practice of their science for my mentees - and an individualized, collaborative (between mentor and mentee) path to success. 
- Dan is available via Slack always during working hours; responsiveness outside of those hours is situation dependent. 
- Bi-weekly individual meeting (at least), feedback on documents within two weeks, IDP development annually.

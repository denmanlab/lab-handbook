# Canon

This is a list of references currently considered to be the lab canon. Note that "canon", when Googled in January 2020, is defined as:
> "a collection or list of sacred books accepted as genuine."

This **does not** mean that these papers are all true, and that every claim made in them is justified and replicated and represent biological reality. However, they have all been important papers in the field or in the lab's thinking, and so they are a part of the canon. [_Question everything._](https://www.yellow5.com/pokey/archive/index161.html)

This is **not** a strictly ordered list. 

In each section one (or more) provisional recent entries are included, which represent relativelt recent works of great interest, whose long-term role in the canon will be decided as the years, and scientific progress, march on.

# Mapping a Network Drive

## Windows 10

 1. Log on to CU Anschutz VPN Portal.
    * [Instructions Here](https://www.ucdenver.edu/offices/office-of-information-technology/software/how-do-i-use/vpn-and-remote-access)
 3. Open File Explorer and click **This PC**.
 4. Click **Map network drive**. This opens the **Map network drive** window.
 5. Choose any letter from the dropdown menu.
 6. In the **Folder** field, enter **\\\DENMANLAB\s1** and click **Finish**.
 7. You'll be prompted to enter your network credentials.
 8. Once you've entered your credentials, a Windows Explorer window. will open with the folder you've just mapped. 

## Mac
 1. Log on to CU Anschutz VPN Portal.
    * [Instructions Here](https://www.ucdenver.edu/offices/office-of-information-technology/software/how-do-i-use/vpn-and-remote-access)
2. Find the menu bar at the top of the screen.
3. Click **Go** and select **Connect to Server**.
4. Enter the hostname for DENMANLAB preceded by "afp://"  (**afp://denmanlab**). Click **Connect** to continue.
5. Enter your credentials. Click **Connect** to continue.
6. You should now see the mapped drive in the **Shared** section of your Finder window. Double-click the folder to explore its contents.
